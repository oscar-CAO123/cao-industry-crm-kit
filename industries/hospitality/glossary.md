# Glossary: Hospitality

Trade terms the hospitality workflows use, in plain words.

**86** (slang): an item that has run out, so it comes off the menu until more arrives. Used by `add-waste`.

**Beverage package**: a fixed price for a set list of drinks for a function. Used by `events`.

**Booked value**: the amount a booking is worth. The venue logs each change to it so booked revenue stays accurate. Used by `bookings`.

**Calendar hold**: a placeholder on the venue calendar that keeps a time free for an enquiry, with no guests invited. Used by `bookings`, `events`.

**Clash**: a status for a request whose time is already taken on the calendar, so no hold is placed. Used by `bookings`, `events`.

**COGS** (cost of goods sold): what the venue paid for the food and drink it sold. Used by `add-waste`.

**Comp**: a free item or meal given with no charge. In the sales records it shows as a discount, often of 100 percent. Used by `add-comps`.

**Deposit**: money taken up front to hold a booking. Used by `bookings`, `events`.

**Dietary requirements**: guests' allergies, intolerances and food preferences, passed to the kitchen before a function. Used by `events`.

**Enquiry**: a request from someone who wants to book a table, room or event, before it is confirmed. Used by `bookings`, `events`.

**Folio**: the guest's running bill in the hotel's system, where room charges and other costs are posted. Used by `tabs-room-charges`.

**Guest tab**: the running bill for a guest who has opened a tab, kept open until they pay. Used by `tabs-room-charges`.

**Labour share**: labour cost as a percentage of sales. Used by `add-labour`.

**Labour target**: the labour share a venue aims to stay under. Used by `add-labour`.

**Night auditor**: the staff member who closes a hotel's accounts for the day, usually overnight. Used by `tabs-room-charges`.

**No show**: a guest with a booking or a waitlist place who does not arrive and does not cancel. Used by `x-guests`.

**Par level**: the minimum stock a venue wants on hand before it reorders. Used by `stock`.

**Physical count**: a stock count done by hand on the shelves or in the cellar, to compare with the system. Used by `stock`.

**POS** (point of sale): the till and payment system a venue rings up its sales on. Used by `add-comps`, `add-waste`, `stock`.

**Quoted wait**: the wait time the waitlist estimates for a party, based on the minutes per group ahead of it. Used by `x-guests`.

**Room charge**: a charge for food, drink or a service that is posted to a hotel guest's folio. Used by `tabs-room-charges`.

**Roster**: the plan of which staff work which shifts. Used by `add-labour`.

**Run sheet**: a timed plan for an event showing what happens and who does it. Used by `events`.

**Set menu**: a fixed menu chosen in advance for a group, rather than ordering from the full menu. Used by `events`.

**Timesheet**: the record of hours each staff member worked, used to work out labour cost. Used by `add-labour`.

**Trial balance**: a report listing the balance of each ledger account, used to check the books agree. Used by `tabs-room-charges`.

**Variance**: the gap between an expected figure and the one that was counted or recorded. Used by `stock`.

**Waitlist**: the list of guests waiting for a table, in the order they joined. Used by `x-guests`.

**Walk-in**: a guest who arrives without a booking. Used by `x-guests`.

**Wastage**: food or drink that is thrown out or lost, logged so its cost can be tracked. Used by `add-waste`.
