# Glossary: Financial Services

Trade terms the financial services workflows use, in plain words.

**Adviser**: A person who gives financial advice to clients, such as on investments, insurance or lending. Used by `documents`, `filenotes` and `x-portfolio`.

**AML** (anti-money laundering): The rules and checks a business follows so its services are not used to hide where money came from. Used by the template's `compliance-checks` workflow.

**Bill**: A supplier's invoice that the business has to pay, as opposed to a sales invoice it sends out. Used by `invoices`.

**Broker**: A business or person who arranges loans or insurance for clients with lenders or insurers. Used by `add-settlement`, `policy`, `policy-renewals` and `x-loans`.

**Chase**: To follow up with someone for money, a document or an answer, usually with a reminder message. Used by `add-settlement`, `documents`, `invoices`, `policy`, `policy-renewals` and `x-loans`.

**Commission**: The payment a broker or adviser receives for arranging a loan or policy, which the lender or insurer pays. Used by `add-settlement`.

**Discharge**: The step where the lender releases its mortgage over a property once the loan has been paid out. Used by `add-settlement`.

**Endorsement**: A change to an existing insurance policy, such as adding a driver or changing a vehicle. Used by `policy-renewals`.

**File note**: A written record of a client conversation, meeting or decision, kept on the client's file. Used by `add-settlement`, `filenotes` and `policy`.

**GST** (Goods and Services Tax): Australia's broad tax on most goods and services. Businesses charge it on their sales and can claim back the GST they pay on purchases. Used by `invoices`.

**Insurer**: The company that issues an insurance policy and pays claims under it. Used by `policy` and `policy-renewals`.

**KYC** (know your customer): The checks that confirm who a client is before a business takes them on. Used by `documents`.

**Lead window**: The period before a policy's renewal date when the pre-renewal email is drafted. Used by `policy-renewals`.

**Lender**: A bank, credit union or other organisation that lends the money for a loan. Used by `add-settlement`, `documents` and `x-loans`.

**Lender confirmation**: The lender's written confirmation that a loan is approved and ready to settle. Used by `add-settlement`.

**Loan register**: The spreadsheet or table where each loan enquiry is logged, with its status. Used by `x-loans`.

**Lodgement**: Sending a finished loan application to a lender for assessment, usually through the lender's own portal. Used by `x-loans`.

**Masking**: Hiding personal details in a copy of a document, such as account numbers or dates of birth, before the copy is shared. Used by `add-mask`.

**Portfolio review**: A scheduled meeting where an adviser goes over a client's goals and financial position and agrees on any changes. Used by `x-portfolio`.

**Post-settlement checks**: The checks made after a loan settles, such as discharge, commission and trust account checks, before the file is closed. The trust account holds client money apart from the business's own money. Used by `add-settlement`.

**Premium**: The amount a policyholder pays the insurer to keep a policy in force. Used by `policy-renewals`.

**Reconciliation**: Matching the transactions in a bank account against the business's own records to check that they agree. Used by `invoices`.

**Review pack**: The summary of a client's details prepared ahead of a portfolio review. Used by `x-portfolio`.

**Serviceability**: Whether a borrower's income and expenses show that they can afford the repayments on a loan. Used by `x-loans`.

**Settlement**: The day a loan is paid out and the transaction completes, such as a property purchase. Used by `add-settlement` and `x-loans`.

**SMSF** (self-managed superannuation fund): A superannuation fund that its own members run as trustees. Used by `invoices`.

**Statement of advice**: The written document that sets out advice given to a client and the reasons for it. It is also known as a record of advice. Used by `x-portfolio`.

**Tax file number**: The number the Australian Taxation Office issues to an individual for tax purposes. Used by `add-mask`.

**Underwriter**: The insurer's staff member or team who assesses a risk and sets the terms and price of cover. Used by `policy-renewals`.
