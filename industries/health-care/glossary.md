# Glossary: Health Care

Trade terms the health care workflows use, in plain words.

**AI scribe**: a tool that listens to a consultation and drafts the clinical note from it. Used by `add-dictation`.

**Budget burn**: how much of a budget line has been spent so far, compared with the plan. Used by `x-budget`.

**Care plan**: a written plan of a patient's goals, supports and treatment, started from their referral. Used by `careplan`.

**Clinical enquiry**: an enquiry about symptoms, treatment or medicine. It goes to a clinician and never gets an automatic reply. Used by `support`.

**Clinician**: a health professional who treats patients and signs off their clinical notes. Used by `add-dictation`, `support`.

**Dictation**: spoken notes turned into written text, so the clinical note does not have to be typed. Used by `add-dictation`.

**DVA** (Department of Veterans' Affairs): the federal department that funds treatment for eligible veterans. Used by `rebates`.

**Funder**: whoever pays for a service, such as a health fund, the NDIS or a government program. Used by `shiftnotes`, `x-agreements`.

**GP** (general practitioner): a family doctor. Used by `results-and-letters`.

**Handover**: the brief a worker gives the next shift about a client's needs and any events. Used by `shiftnotes`.

**Health fund**: a private health insurer, which can pay rebates for some services. Used by `rebates`.

**Holding reply**: a short reply that confirms an enquiry has been received and when a full answer will come. It gives no clinical advice. Used by `support`.

**Incident record**: a written record of an event that harmed, or could have harmed, a client or worker. Used by `shiftnotes`.

**Item number**: a code that identifies a billable service on a claim. Used by `add-dictation`.

**Lock** (of a note): finalising a clinical note so it can no longer be changed. A clinician approves each lock. Used by `add-dictation`.

**Medicare**: Australia's public health insurance scheme, which pays rebates for some services a clinic provides. Used by `rebates`, `support`.

**Medication log**: a record of medicines given to a client, and when. Used by `shiftnotes`.

**NDIS** (National Disability Insurance Scheme): the Australian government scheme that funds supports for people with disability.

**Patient case**: one episode of care on a patient's record, such as a course of treatment. Used by `x-agreements`.

**Plan manager**: a service that manages an NDIS participant's plan funds and pays their providers. Used by `shiftnotes`, `x-agreements`.

**Rebate**: money a government or health fund pays back towards the cost of a service. Used by `rebates`.

**Recall**: a scheduled reminder that brings a patient back for a review. Used by `add-dictation`, `results-and-letters`.

**Referral**: a letter from a doctor or other provider sending a patient for treatment or assessment. Used by `careplan`, `results-and-letters`.

**Service agreement**: a written agreement between the practice and a patient, setting out the services and terms. Used by `x-agreements`.

**Shift note**: the written record a support worker makes after a shift, saying what support was given. Used by `shiftnotes`.

**Support worker**: a worker who helps a client with daily tasks and activities. Used by `shiftnotes`.

**Timesheet**: the record of hours a worker has worked, used for pay and billing. Used by `shiftnotes`.

**Treatment note**: the written clinical record of a patient visit, kept in the practice system. Used by `add-dictation`, `careplan`, `results-and-letters`.

**Variance**: the gap between a planned figure and the actual figure. Used by `x-budget`.

**Waitlist**: a list of patients who want an earlier appointment, offered a slot when one opens. Used by `add-reminders`.
