# Glossary: Automotive

Trade terms the automotive workflows use, in plain words.

**Amount financed**: The sum borrowed under a finance deal. The proposal states it as an amount, and any rate or payment is only an estimate the dealer confirms. Used by `deal-structuring`.

**Booking diary**: The workshop's calendar of booked visits. The service reminders do not book visits, so a person books them in the diary. Used by `service`.

**Chargeback**: Money taken back from a sale after it has been paid. The commission workflow does not yet net chargebacks off commission. Used by `commission`.

**Commission tiers**: The bands of sales that set the commission rate, with a higher rate at each level. The tiers in this kit are an assumption to confirm with the client. Used by `commission`.

**Consent on file**: The customer's agreement to a credit check and to the application going to lenders, recorded before a proposal is marked ready. Used by `deal-structuring`.

**Credit application**: The customer's finance application. It must be complete before a deal proposal is written. Used by `deal-structuring`.

**Deal structuring**: Working out which lenders a vehicle finance deal goes to, and for what amount and term, before any lender receives it. Used by `deal-structuring`.

**Diagnostic write-up**: A plain description of a fault and the work it needs, written for the customer from a technician's notes and photos. Used by `diag`.

**Discharge**: Removing a registered security interest once the debt is paid. The PPSR workflow reminds a person, and a discharge is due within 5 business days of the interest ending. Used by `ppsr`.

**Due list**: The list of customers whose next service falls due within the reminder window, built from their completed jobs. Used by `service`.

**Fault code**: A code a vehicle's computer stores when it detects a fault. A technician reads it with a scan tool and types it into the job notes, because the kit does not read scan tools. Used by `diag`.

**Finance pack**: The set of documents sent to one lender for a deal. The workflow drafts one pack per chosen lender, and a person approves each send. Used by `deal-structuring`.

**Grantor search**: A PPSR search by the name of the party who gave the security interest, used when a search by the vehicle's serial number is not possible. Used by `ppsr`.

**Lender**: A finance company that funds a vehicle loan or lease. Each lender has its own submission address in the workflow settings. Used by `deal-structuring`.

**Listing copy**: The written description of a vehicle that goes on a sales listing. It states only what the owner has confirmed, and a person pastes the approved copy into the listing site. Used by `vehicle-listing`.

**Loan term**: The length of a finance deal in months. Used by `deal-structuring`.

**Match text**: Text placed in an invoice's Reference so the commission workflow can match a sale to a salesperson. Invoices carry no sales rep field. Used by `commission`.

**NEVDIS** (National Exchange of Vehicle and Driver Information System): The national database that holds vehicle registration records and written-off or stolen vehicle data. The PPSR register shows NEVDIS data where it is available. Used by `ppsr`.

**Ownership transfer**: Changing a vehicle's registered owner to the buyer. Once a vehicle is sold, the listing workflow drafts a checklist for the transfer desk and never puts buyer details in an email. Used by `vehicle-listing`.

**Pay run**: A payroll run that pays staff for one period. Each month's commission goes onto a draft pay run that a person approves and posts. Used by `commission`.

**PPSR** (Personal Property Securities Register): The national register where security interests in vehicles and other goods are recorded and searched. Used by `ppsr`.

**Recall**: A manufacturer's request for owners to bring in a vehicle to fix a safety defect. The service workflow does not check for recalls yet. Used by `service`.

**Rego**: The registration number on a vehicle's number plate. Used by `ppsr`.

**Search certificate**: The document a PPSR search produces, listing the security interests registered against a vehicle. A person reads it before a buy or hold decision. Used by `ppsr`.

**Security interest**: A lender's registered claim over a vehicle while money is still owed on it. Used by `ppsr`.

**Service interval**: The time between a vehicle's scheduled services. The workflow sets it in months from the customer's last completed job. Used by `service`.

**Service reminder**: A message telling a customer that their next service is due. A person approves each reminder before it is sent. Used by `service`.

**Stock register**: The dealer's list of vehicles for sale, with stock number, price, status and photo folder. The listing and PPSR workflows read it. Used by `vehicle-listing`, `ppsr`.

**VIN** (Vehicle Identification Number): The unique number that identifies a vehicle. A VIN search on the PPSR is the usual check for a motor vehicle. Used by `ppsr`.
