# Glossary: Retail

Trade terms the retail workflows use, in plain words.

**Artwork**: the finished design files for a product, used to make its mockups and print. Used by `artwork`.

**Batch number**: a code that groups items made or received together, so a faulty batch can be traced. Used by `x-batches`.

**Chargeback**: a card payment a customer disputes with their bank, which the bank can take back from the business. Used by `disputes`.

**Chase**: a follow-up email or reminder for an overdue invoice. Used by `wholesale`.

**Credit note**: a document that reduces what a customer owes, used to record a refund or adjustment in the accounts system. Used by `disputes`.

**D2C** (direct to consumer): a business that sells straight to its own customers, not through shops. Used by `x-flows`.

**Dispatch**: sending a finished order out of the warehouse to a customer or stockist. Used by `fulfil`, `wholesale`.

**Dispute evidence**: the facts and records a business sends to a bank to contest a chargeback. Used by `disputes`.

**Draft invoice**: an invoice that is saved but not yet approved or sent. Used by `orders`, `wholesale`.

**Email flow**: a set of automated emails sent when a customer does something, such as signing up or leaving a cart. Used by `x-flows`.

**Fulfilment**: the steps from taking an order to shipping it out, including stock checks and picking. Used by `fulfil`.

**GST** (goods and services tax): Australia's tax on most sales. It affects how refunds and credits are recorded. Used by `disputes`.

**Landed cost**: the total cost of getting stock to the warehouse, including freight and surcharges, on top of the purchase price. Used by `x-batches`.

**Licensor**: the company that owns a brand or character and must approve how it appears on a product. Used by `supplier-production`.

**Listing**: a product's page on the store, with its images, text and price. Used by `artwork`.

**Low stock**: stock at or below a set level, flagged so it can be reordered. Used by `products`.

**Mockup**: a picture showing artwork on the finished product. Used by `artwork`.

**Needs-lines**: the state an order is held in when it has no line proposal yet, so no invoice is drafted. Used by `orders`, `supplier-production`.

**Payment terms**: how many days a customer has to pay an invoice. Used by `wholesale`.

**PO** (purchase order): a written order for goods, sent to a supplier or factory at an agreed price. Used by `supplier-production`, `x-batches`.

**Production board**: the shared board where each factory order is tracked from start to delivery. Used by `supplier-production`.

**Refund**: money returned to a customer for a sale or a return. Used by `disputes`.

**Retirement**: taking a product out of sale and stock for good. Used by `products`.

**Serial number**: a unique number given to one item, so that single item can be tracked. Used by `x-batches`.

**SKU** (stock keeping unit): the code a business uses to identify each product variant it sells. Used by `fulfil`, `products`.

**Sprint**: the work item opened on one weak email flow, to build its replacement. Used by `x-flows`.

**Stock on hand**: the quantity of an item the system shows as currently in stock. Used by `fulfil`, `products`.

**Stockist**: a shop or retailer that stocks the business's products and buys them at trade prices. Used by `wholesale`.

**Tech pack**: a spec sheet a factory works from, with measurements, materials and construction for a product. Used by `supplier-production`.

**Trade price**: the price set for wholesale buyers, below the retail price. Used by `wholesale`.
