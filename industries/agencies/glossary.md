# Glossary: Agencies

Trade terms the agencies workflows use, in plain words.

**Account manager**: The person who looks after a client day to day, and who reads and approves what goes to that client. Used by `report` and `adaudit`.

**Ad account**: The account on an ad platform under which a client's ads run and spend money. Used by `adaudit` and `creative`.

**Ad audit**: A daily check of a client's live ad accounts against agreed thresholds, to catch spend that is not working. Used by `adaudit`.

**Brief**: A client's written request for a job, which the agency scopes and prices before the work starts. Used by `ops`, `creative` and `x-jobbudgets`.

**Burn**: How much of a job's estimate or budget has been spent so far. Used by `x-jobbudgets`.

**Change request**: A written request to change a job's scope or budget, priced and agreed with the client before the extra work starts. Used by `x-jobbudgets`.

**Commentary**: The short written explanation of a client's monthly numbers: what moved, why it probably moved, and what happens next. Used by `report`.

**Conversion event**: An action a person takes after seeing an ad, such as a purchase or a lead, which the ad platform counts as a result. Used by `adaudit`.

**Creative**: The ad itself, meaning its images, video and copy, as distinct from who sees it or how much is spent. Used by `adaudit` and `creative`.

**CTR** (click-through rate): The share of times an ad is shown that someone clicks it, shown as a percentage. Used by `adaudit` and `creative`.

**Deal**: A sales opportunity in the business's sales system. Once it reaches the brief status, it becomes a new brief to scope. Used by `ops`.

**Estimate**: The hours and fee a job is priced at before the work starts, which spend is checked against. Used by `ops` and `x-jobbudgets`.

**Frequency**: The average number of times each person in an audience has seen an ad. Used by `adaudit`.

**Hook**: The opening line or image of an ad, written to stop someone scrolling past it. Used by `creative`.

**Impressions**: The number of times an ad was shown on screen. Used by `adaudit` and `creative`.

**Job budget**: The amount approved for a client job, which the spend on that job is checked against. Used by `x-jobbudgets`.

**Overrun**: Spend on a job that goes past its estimate or budget. Used by `x-jobbudgets`.

**Pacing**: How evenly an ad budget is spent across the days of a period. Used by `adaudit`.

**Pause**: To stop an ad or campaign running, so that it stops spending. Used by `adaudit` and `creative`.

**Pixel**: A small piece of code on a website that records visits and actions for an ad platform, so the platform can measure results. Used by `adaudit`.

**Reforecast**: A revised estimate of the hours or cost a job will need, set after the work has started. Used by `x-jobbudgets`.

**Scope**: The work a client has agreed to buy, and what is left out. Used by `ops` and `x-jobbudgets`.

**Scope creep**: Extra work added to a job beyond the agreed scope, often without a change to the price. Used by `x-jobbudgets`.

**Variant**: A version of an ad or creative made to test against another version. Used by `creative`.
