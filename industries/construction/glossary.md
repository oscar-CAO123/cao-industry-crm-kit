# Glossary: Construction

Trade terms the construction workflows use, in plain words.

**Allowance**: a sum set in the contract for an item the client chooses, such as tiles or fittings. Used by `x-selections`.

**Authorised**: a status meaning a person has approved an invoice or purchase order to go out. A draft has not been approved yet. Used by `wip`, `procurement`, `x-stock`.

**Backcharge**: a charge passed on to a subcontractor for the cost of fixing their work. Used by `defects`.

**Close-out**: the final stage of a job, once every defect is fixed and the handover paperwork is ready. Used by `defects`, `safety`.

**Condition of consent**: a requirement set in a council approval that must be met before or during the work. Used by `approvals`.

**Ex GST**: a price with GST not included. Used by `wip`, `procurement`.

**Expression of interest**: a short written reply saying the business wants to bid for a tender. Used by `tenders`.

**Extension of time claim**: a written request to the client for more time to finish a job, usually because of delays outside the business's control. Used by `paperwork`.

**Foreman**: the site supervisor who runs the crew on a job. Used by `x-rfis`, `variations`.

**Go or no-go**: the decision on whether the business will bid for a tender, based on whether the work fits its size and timing. Used by `tenders`, `takeoff`.

**LGC** (large-scale generation certificate): a certificate created for eligible renewable electricity from an accredited large power station. Used by `env-certificates`.

**Markup**: a note or shape added to a plan file, which the takeoff reads to build its rows. Used by `takeoff`.

**Plant**: the machines and equipment a business owns or hires, such as excavators or cranes. Used by `x-plant`.

**PO** (purchase order): a written order to a supplier stating what is wanted and the agreed price. Used by `procurement`, `x-stock`.

**Pre-start**: a checklist a crew completes before starting work on a job. Used by `safety`.

**Progress claim**: an invoice for the work done on a job so far, sent at set intervals under the contract. Used by `wip`.

**Punch item**: an item on a job's list for a defect or unfinished task that the trade must fix before sign-off. Used by `defects`.

**Rate card**: the list of a business's prices, each with a code, used to price quotes and variations. Used by `quoting`, `variations`.

**Retention**: a share of each progress claim that the client holds back, to be released later, usually at completion. Used by `wip`.

**RFI** (request for information): a formal question from the site to the designer about what the drawings or specifications mean. Used by `x-rfis`.

**Selections**: the client's choices of tiles, fittings, colours and other options, kept against each allowance. Used by `x-selections`.

**STC** (small-scale technology certificate): a certificate created for eligible small-scale installations such as solar systems, under the federal small-scale renewable energy scheme. Used by `env-certificates`.

**Subcontractor**: a trade business hired to do part of a job, also known as a subbie. Used by `x-subbies`, `x-labour`.

**SWMS** (safe work method statement): a written plan for a high-risk task that names the hazards and how they will be controlled. Used by `safety`.

**Takeoff**: the list of quantities measured from the plans to price a job. Used by `takeoff`.

**Tender**: a formal invitation to bid for a job, with a closing date. Used by `tenders`, `takeoff`.

**Trade**: a specialist group of workers, such as plumbers, electricians or painters. Used by `defects`, `procurement`.

**Variation**: extra work or a change to the contract, priced and approved by the client before it is done. Used by `variations`, `wip`.

**VEEC** (Victorian Energy Efficiency Certificate): a certificate created under the Victorian energy efficiency scheme for eligible energy-saving work in Victoria. Used by `env-certificates`.

**WIP** (work in progress): the position of each job, comparing the value of the work done with what has been billed for it. Used by `wip`.
