# Glossary: Fitness Beauty

Trade terms the fitness beauty workflows use, in plain words.

**Access hold**: pausing a member's bookings or membership until an unpaid account is settled. Used by `x-billing`.

**Baseline measurements**: the first measurements or fitness tests taken before a client starts a program, so progress can be measured against them. Used by `client-programs`.

**Chase**: following up an unpaid invoice or failed payment with reminders. Used by `x-billing`.

**Check-in**: a message sent to a client who has gone a set number of days without a logged workout. Used by `client-programs`.

**Class timetable**: the schedule of classes, with times, instructors and rooms, kept in the booking system. Used by `x-timetable`.

**Client intake**: the form a new client fills in with their goals, injuries, history and schedule. Used by `client-programs`.

**Client program**: a workout, meal or habit plan written for one client and tracked against their goals. Used by `client-programs`.

**Deferred income**: money paid for services not yet delivered, held as a liability until it is used. Used by `x-packs`.

**Deposit**: money taken up front to hold a booking.

**Failed payment**: a card charge that was declined, or an invoice still unpaid past its due date. Used by `x-billing`.

**Fill rate**: the share of a class's places that are booked. Used by `x-timetable`.

**Fill target**: the fill rate the owner wants each class to reach. Classes under it are flagged. Used by `x-timetable`.

**Gift card**: a prepaid card a customer buys to spend later, held as a balance. Used by `x-packs`.

**Instructor**: the coach or trainer who runs a class. Used by `x-timetable`.

**Membership**: a recurring plan a member pays for to keep their access. Used by `x-billing`.

**No-show**: a booked client who does not arrive and does not cancel.

**Pack**: a bundle of prepaid classes or visits that a client uses up over time. Used by `x-packs`.

**Retry**: a second attempt to charge a card that was declined. Used by `x-billing`.

**Room block**: a booking that takes a room out of the timetable for a set time. Used by `x-timetable`.

**SOP** (standard operating procedure): a written, step-by-step guide to a routine task, used to train staff.

**Trial balance**: a report listing the balance of each ledger account, used to check the books agree. Used by `x-packs`.

**Win-back**: an effort to bring back a client or member who has stopped coming.
