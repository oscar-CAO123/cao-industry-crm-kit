# Glossary: Real Estate

Trade terms the real estate workflows use, in plain words.

**Allocation**: assigning a maintenance job to a named maintenance person. Used by `add-maint`.

**Appraisal**: a valuation of a property, usually for a seller, that estimates what it might sell for. Used by `pipeline`.

**Arrears**: rent that is overdue and still unpaid, chased in steps until it is cleared. Used by `add-arrears`.

**Bond**: the deposit a tenant pays when a tenancy starts. It is held by a state bond authority once lodged. Used by `bond-lodgement`.

**Cash flow**: what is left of the rent after costs and loan interest are paid. Used by `investment`.

**Comparable sales**: recent sales of similar nearby properties, used to judge what a property is worth. Used by `investment`.

**Condition report**: a written record, with photos, of a property's condition at a point in time, such as the start or end of a tenancy. Used by `condition`.

**Conveyancer**: the person who handles the legal transfer of a property for the buyer or seller. Used by `settlement`.

**Cooling-off**: a short period after a contract is signed in which a buyer can withdraw. The length and rules vary by state. Used by `settlement`.

**Exit inspection**: the inspection at the end of a tenancy that decides what is kept from the bond. Used by `bond-lodgement`.

**Gross yield**: the annual rent as a percentage of the property price, before costs are taken out. Used by `investment`.

**Guarantor**: a person who promises to cover the tenant's rent if the tenant does not pay. Used by `tenant-screening`.

**Maintenance request**: a repair request from a tenant or owner, logged as a job before anyone is sent to do the work. Used by `add-maint`.

**Marketing consent**: a recorded yes that an owner agreed to receive marketing email. Some property systems store it in a field named after the European GDPR rules. An owner with no record is never emailed. Used by `owners`.

**Notice to remedy**: a written notice that gives a tenant a set time to fix a breach of the lease. Used by `add-arrears`.

**Open home**: a scheduled time when a property for sale or lease is open for people to walk through without an appointment. Used by `add-vendor`.

**Payment plan**: a written arrangement for a tenant to repay arrears in set amounts. Used by `add-arrears`.

**Portal enquiry**: a message from someone who saw a listing on a property website. Used by `listing-syndication`.

**Principal**: the licensed person who runs a real estate agency and is responsible for its compliance, including its trust account. Used by `add-trust`.

**Property manager**: the person who looks after rental properties for owners, including rent, repairs and tenant mail. Used by `pminbox`.

**Routine inspection**: a scheduled visit to check a rented property's condition, with notice given to the tenant. Used by `x-rentalcompliance`.

**Safety certificate**: a record that a required safety check on a rental has been done. Used by `x-rentalcompliance`.

**Settlement**: the day a property sale completes, when the buyer pays and takes ownership. Used by `settlement`.

**Smoke alarm check**: a check of a rental's smoke alarms, done on a set cycle. Used by `x-rentalcompliance`.

**Syndication**: sending one listing to several property portals at once. Used by `listing-syndication`.

**Tenancy**: a tenant's lease on a property, from its start date to its end date. Used by `bond-lodgement`, `tenant-screening`.

**Tenant screening**: the checks on an applicant before a lease is signed, such as ID, income, references and credit. Used by `tenant-screening`.

**Trust account**: a bank account an agency holds other people's money in, kept apart from its own money. Used by `add-trust`.

**Vacancy**: a rental with no current tenant, ready to be listed. Used by `listing-syndication`.

**Vendor**: the owner who is selling a property, and who receives the vendor report. Used by `add-vendor`.
