# Glossary: Professional Services

Trade terms the professional services workflows use, in plain words.

**AML** (anti-money laundering): The rules and checks a firm follows so its services are not used to hide where money came from. Used by the template's `compliance-checks` workflow.

**ATO** (Australian Taxation Office): The Australian Government agency that administers the tax system, including the BAS and PAYG. Used by `add-bas` and `x-duedates`.

**Bank feed**: The automatic link that brings a bank account's transactions into the accounting software. Used by `add-bas` and `add-missingdocs`.

**BAS** (Business Activity Statement): The return a business lodges with the ATO to report its GST and other tax, usually each quarter. Used by `add-bas` and `x-duedates`.

**Bill**: A supplier's invoice that the business has to pay. Used by `add-missingdocs`.

**Case law**: Decisions by courts that show how the law has been applied, which lawyers cite in their work. Used by `litigation-drafting`.

**Chargeable time**: Time that a practice can bill to a client. Used by `add-narratives`.

**Coding**: Assigning each transaction to the right account and tax type in the accounting software. Used by `add-bas`.

**Conflict check**: A check, before a firm takes on a matter, that it does not already act for someone whose interests clash with the new client's. Used by `add-conflict`.

**Cost per lead**: The ad spend divided by the number of leads the ads produced. Used by `marketing`.

**Disbursement**: An expense a firm pays to a third party on a client's behalf, such as a court fee, paid from the client's trust money or recovered on the bill. Used by `x-trust`.

**Engagement letter**: The letter that sets out the scope, fee and terms on which a firm will act, which the client accepts before work starts. Used by `add-proposal`.

**Ethical wall**: A barrier that keeps information about one matter away from certain staff, to manage a conflict. Used by `add-conflict`.

**Fee earner**: A staff member whose time is recorded and billed to clients, such as a lawyer or accountant. Used by `x-autotime`.

**GST** (Goods and Services Tax): Australia's broad tax on most goods and services. Businesses charge it on their sales and claim back the GST they pay on purchases. Used by `add-bas` and `add-proposal`.

**Lead**: An enquiry that has not yet been opened as a matter. Used by `add-conflict`, `litigation-drafting` and `x-matter`.

**Lodge**: To send a return or form to a government agency, such as the ATO. Used by `add-bas` and `x-duedates`.

**Matter**: A client job or case that a firm opens and tracks, such as a case, a transaction or an engagement. Used by `add-conflict`, `litigation-drafting`, `x-autotime`, `x-matter` and `x-trust`.

**Narrative**: The plain-language description of the work on one invoice line for a client. Used by `add-narratives`.

**Partner**: A senior owner of a firm who approves key decisions, such as opening a matter or signing off a return. Used by `add-bas` and `add-conflict`.

**PAYG** (Pay As You Go): The system under which tax is withheld from wages and paid to the ATO during the year, and under which businesses pay their tax in instalments. Used by `x-duedates`.

**Pixel**: A small piece of code on a website that records visits and actions for an ad platform, so the platform can measure results. Used by `marketing`.

**Pleading**: A formal document filed in court that sets out one party's claim, or its response to a claim. Used by `litigation-drafting`.

**Pleading matrix**: A table that lists each allegation in a claim, the response to it (admit, deny or no knowledge) and the reason for that response. Used by `litigation-drafting`.

**Practice**: A law firm, accounting firm or other professional services firm. Used by `add-bas`, `add-conflict` and `add-missingdocs`.

**Source document**: The bill, receipt or invoice that supports a transaction in the books. Used by `add-bas`.

**Trust account**: A separate bank account that holds client money, apart from the firm's own money. Used by `x-trust`.

**WIP** (work in progress): Time and costs recorded on matters that have not yet been invoiced. Used by `add-narratives`.

**Write-off**: Removing unbilled time or an unpaid amount from the books, so that it is not charged or chased. Used by `add-narratives` and `x-autotime`.
