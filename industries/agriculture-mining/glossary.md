# Glossary: Agriculture Mining

Trade terms the agriculture and mining workflows use, in plain words.

**Carbon credit**: A unit a verified carbon project can earn for storing carbon or cutting emissions. The workflow gathers the farm records a project would need, but registration, claims and sale stay with the business. Used by `carbon-credits`.

**Commercial invoice**: The invoice that travels with an export shipment, showing the goods, their value and the buyer. The kit drafts a sales invoice, so confirm the format with the buyer or forwarder. Used by `shipdocs`.

**Consignee**: The party who receives an export shipment at its destination. Used by `shipdocs`.

**Cost of production**: The total cost of growing or raising one unit, such as a tonne, a hectare or a head, worked out from the books. Used by `x-costprod`.

**Docket**: The paper slip that comes with a delivery, recording the spec, grade and weight of the load. The ticket workflow takes each docket's details from a photo or scan, which a person enters. Used by `tickets`.

**Exceedance**: A reading or lab result that is over its licence or site limit. The workflow lists it for a person, and the owner decides what it means and what to do. Used by `environmental-monitoring`.

**Grade**: The quality class a load is paid at. The ticket check compares each docket's grade with the grade the contract pays for its spec, and queues a QC email when they differ. Used by `tickets`, `spray`.

**Grower statement**: A statement sent to a grower showing what their produce sold for, less the packing, freight and levy charges. Used by `packout`.

**Head**: One animal, the unit used to count and cost livestock. Used by `x-costprod`.

**Label rate**: The amount of product to apply per hectare, as set on the product label. The spray and paddock records carry it, but the kit does not check it against the label. Used by `spray`, `x-paddocks`.

**Lot**: A batch code that groups produce through the shed, so each lot's weights can be followed. Used by `yield`, `shipdocs`.

**Net tonnes**: The weight of the product alone, without the truck or container, in tonnes. In this kit it comes only from what the docket states. Used by `tickets`.

**NLIS** (National Livestock Identification System): The national system that identifies livestock by their tag number and records their movements. The NVD workflow matches each request's tags to the farm's stock register. Used by `livestock-nvds`.

**NVD** (National Vendor Declaration): The declaration a livestock seller completes when animals are sold or moved. It sets out the animals, their treatments and withholding periods, and the vendor signs it. Used by `livestock-nvds`.

**Pack-out**: The saleable output a grower's produce gives once packed, with its value and the charges taken from it. Used by `packout`.

**Packhouse**: The shed where produce is washed, graded and packed for sale. Used by `packout`, `yield`.

**Packing list**: A list of the cartons or pallets in a shipment and what each one holds. Used by `shipdocs`.

**Paddock**: A named area of land, such as a grazing or cropping field, that farm records are kept against. Used by `x-paddocks`.

**PIC** (Property Identification Code): The code for the property where livestock are kept. The workflow does not check it, so a person checks it on each NVD. Used by `livestock-nvds`.

**Pool pricing**: A pool sells several growers' produce together and pays each grower from the combined sale. Used by `packout`.

**QC** (quality control): The check of a record or load before it is accepted. The QC email lists each item a person needs to check. Used by `spray`, `tickets`, `x-paddocks`.

**Rain gauge**: The instrument that measures rainfall in millimetres. Each rainfall reading is entered against the farm's gauge. Used by `crop-records`.

**Receival**: Taking in a delivery of grain or produce at a site and recording its weight and grade. Used by `spray`, `tickets`.

**Spray record**: The written record of each spray: product, rate, field, date, weather at application and the date the withholding period ends. Used by `spray`, `x-paddocks`.

**Stock register**: The farm's list of animals and their groups, read from the farm's records and used to match NVD tags. Used by `livestock-nvds`.

**Trailing average**: The average of recent months' invoiced volume, used as the base forecast. The owner adjusts it for the season and for what buyers say. Used by `market-forecasting`.

**Weighbridge**: A large scale that a truck drives onto to be weighed. The kit does not read weighbridge weights, so net tonnes come from the docket. Used by `tickets`.

**Withholding period**: The time that must pass after a treatment or spray before the produce or animal can be sold or harvested. The product label or treatment record sets it, and the kit does not calculate it. Used by `spray`, `x-paddocks`, `livestock-nvds`.

**Yield**: The packed weight as a share of the gross weight of a lot. Waste is the waste weight as a share of gross, and a lot below its yield target or above its waste limit is raised as an exception. Used by `yield`.
