# Glossary: Transport

Trade terms the transport workflows use, in plain words.

**Access permit**: a permit, issued through the NHVR portal, that lets a heavy vehicle use a restricted road or route. Used by `mass-compliance`.

**ATO** (Australian Taxation Office): the federal tax office that a business reports to through its BAS. Used by `fuel-tax-credits`.

**Authorised**: a status meaning a person has approved an invoice to go out. A draft has not been approved yet. Used by `invoices`.

**Axle group**: one or more axles counted together when working out a vehicle's mass limits. Used by `mass-compliance`.

**BAS** (business activity statement): the regular return a business lodges with the ATO, reporting GST and other tax amounts. Used by `fuel-tax-credits`.

**Bill**: an invoice from a supplier that the business has to pay, such as a fuel account. Used by `fuel-tax-credits`.

**CAN fault** (controller area network): a fault code reported by a vehicle's onboard electronics. Used by `fleet`.

**Carrier**: a transport company that moves freight for the business and invoices it for the work.

**Con note** (consignment note): the document that travels with a load, naming the goods and where they are going.

**Dispatch**: sending a route or set of stops out to a driver or team. Used by `x-routes`.

**Docket**: the delivery slip a receiver signs as proof that a load arrived. Used by `invoices`.

**Fleet service**: a scheduled service for a vehicle, booked when it falls due. Used by `fleet`.

**Fuel tax credit**: a credit for the fuel tax included in fuel a business uses, claimed through the BAS. The rate depends on how the fuel is used, for example off public roads. Used by `fuel-tax-credits`.

**GCM** (gross combination mass): the maximum loaded weight of a vehicle and its trailers together. Used by `mass-compliance`.

**GST** (goods and services tax): a tax on most sales in Australia, reported through the BAS. Used by `fuel-tax-credits`, `invoices`.

**GVM** (gross vehicle mass): the maximum loaded weight a vehicle is rated to carry. Used by `mass-compliance`.

**Mass limit**: the most a heavy vehicle or combination may weigh, in total and by axle group. Used by `mass-compliance`.

**NHVR** (National Heavy Vehicle Regulator): the national regulator for heavy vehicles, which issues mass and access permits. Used by `mass-compliance`.

**Off-road litres**: the litres of fuel used somewhere other than public roads, which the claim is worked out from. Used by `fuel-tax-credits`.

**POD** (proof of delivery): the signed docket, photo or signature that shows a stop was completed. Used by `x-pod`, `invoices`.

**Rate card**: the list of a business's prices, with a code for each item, used to price invoices. Used by `invoices`.

**RCTI** (recipient created tax invoice): a tax invoice the business creates for work a driver or subcontractor did for it, in place of one from the subcontractor. Both sides agree to this in writing first. Used by `invoices`.

**Re-attempt**: a second delivery try for a stop that failed. Used by `x-pod`.

**Stop**: one drop-off or pick-up point on a route. Used by `x-routes`, `x-pod`.

**Surcharge**: an extra charge added to a freight invoice, such as for access or a fuel adjustment. Used by `invoices`.

**Telematics**: data a vehicle sends from its GPS and sensors to a fleet platform. Used by `fuel-tax-credits`.

**Work diary**: the driver's record of work and rest time, used to check fatigue rules. Used by `x-routes`.
